GLOBAL REQUIREMENTS FOR ALL PAGES
================================================

- Use the same Finwert brand system: navy, white, blue and subtle gold.
- Keep typography, buttons, cards and spacing consistent across pages.
- Add a sticky header and a strong mobile navigation.
- Use clear CTA buttons: Schedule a Consultation, Talk to an Expert,
  Explore Services.
- Use real Finwert contact details only; do not use template or dummy data.
- Add descriptive image alt text for accessibility and SEO.
- Add page-specific SEO title, meta description and Open Graph image.
- Add proper internal links between related services and contact page.
- Add forms with success and error states.
- Make phone numbers and email addresses clickable.
- Keep service descriptions concise on listing pages and detailed on service
  pages.
- Add a reusable footer with service links, contact details, legal links and
  copyright information.
- Confirm whether the 75+ professional figure and all phone numbers are still
  current before publishing.
